What goes in the notebooks folder?

We will put our Jupyter notebook 01_data_exploration.ipynb here.
A notebook is a file that ends in .ipynb. It has small blocks called
cells. Some cells contain Python code. Others contain notes. When we
run a code cell, its result appears below it as a table or a chart.

Why do we use a notebook?

Our notebook will help us understand the data and explain our work.
It will open data/phishing_email.csv and count the two classes.
A chart will show how many emails are legitimate and how many are
spam or phishing.

We will also check how many rows have missing text or repeated messages.
This helps us explain why the training script cleans the data.

How will we show the model results?

Our notebook will open results/metrics.csv and make a chart of the scores.
This will show the differences between Naive Bayes and logistic
regression. We will add short notes to explain what the scores mean.

The model comparison runs through train.py. Our notebook will show
the data checks and model results with tables, charts and notes.
